# Before TestFlight

{: .no_toc }

What to know before you install a test build, and what you are agreeing to help with.

1. TOC
{:toc}

## What a test build is

Outpost is not on the App Store yet. A TestFlight build is the real app, signed and installed
through Apple's TestFlight, with two differences worth knowing: it is a build that has not been
through App Review, and it is a build where something can be wrong.

Everything the app claims about privacy is true of a test build. There is no extra telemetry in it,
no analytics, and no crash reporting that reaches the developer with your content in it. What Apple
collects from TestFlight is Apple's, and is described in TestFlight itself.

## What you need

- An iPhone, iPad or Mac that can run the current OS release.
- An Apple Account signed into iCloud, with iCloud Drive on. Outpost keeps your messages in your own
  iCloud storage, so without iCloud there is nothing for it to write to.
- Somebody else who is also testing. Outpost is a messaging app with no server and no directory: a
  single tester on their own can look at the screens, and nothing else.

## What to expect

- **Your history lives on your devices and in your own iCloud.** If you delete the app and your
  iCloud data, it is gone. There is no server copy to restore from.
- **Save your recovery key when the app offers it.** It is the only way back into your identity if
  you lose the device, and nobody can reissue it.
- **A test build can be replaced by a later one that cannot read what the earlier one wrote.** It
  should not happen, and it is the kind of thing testing is for. Do not put anything in a test build
  you would be upset to lose.

## What you get

Everyone who tests before release gets a free year of Outpost Supporter once the app is out. You
claim it from the You screen, and it starts on the day the app reaches the App Store rather than the
day you claim it.
