Outpost · design pass · specifications

Design blockers and specifications

What is still open on the design side, the exact values every board draws from, and the accessibility requirement for each of the 84 boards. Component rulings and the changes they ask of the app live in the decision record; this document is only the part that is mine to answer.

One blocker remains. Everything the earlier version of this page listed under contrast has been drawn and is specified below rather than pending.

Open blockers

D2 · Three flows confirmed but never re-read against the app

Boards 23 to 25, the multi-step leave, were confirmed as still wanted. But the app currently leaves on a swipe with no questions asked, so the drawn flow has never been checked against what leaving now does — the steps may describe consequences the code no longer produces. Boards 27 to 30, recovery through nominated friends and the in-app lock, are unbuilt and were confirmed on their own terms rather than against anything.

Neither blocks a submission. Both should be read against the app before they are built, which is a session with the code rather than a drawing task.

Use exactly this

Every value the boards are drawn at. Where a value is derived rather than fixed, the derivation is given instead of a hex, because it has to hold at seven accents in two appearances.

Accent tokens

--acThe accent's dark value. Text, icons, edges, the unread dot, selection rings. Floor 3:1, which all seven clear.
--acLThe accent's separately measured light value. Same roles, light appearance.
--acFSolid fills that carry white text. Derived: scale the dark value's channels down until white reaches 4.6:1. Never a hand-picked hex. Filled buttons, sent bubbles.
--acFLSame derivation from the light value.
--acT / --acTLTint at 22% dark, 13% light. Incoming bubbles, avatar discs, chips.
--acWWash at 14%. The optional bar tint only. Ships off.

Accessibility, board by board

Four rules hold everywhere and are not repeated per board: no state is carried by colour alone, every layout is built from system text styles and survives to the largest accessibility size, no control is under 44pt, and nothing announces a count where a sentence is what a person needs. The column below is what each board adds to those.

Where a board's layout cannot survive the largest sizes, the alternate arrangement is named rather than left to whoever builds it.

Turn 10 · rulings applied

26 Remove a memberAll three facts are spoken before either button. The check and info glyphs are decorative; the sentences carry the meaning.

91 Light mode rulesReference sheet. Every swatch has its value as text beside it, so the specification does not depend on seeing colour.

Closed this pass

White text on solid accent fill was the one open design blocker and it is drawn: 77 dark fills, 7 light fills and the three destructive buttons now sit on derived values that clear 4.5:1 at every accent in both appearances, and the derivation rather than the resulting hexes is what is specified above. Cobalt, which an earlier retint had collapsed onto verdigris in both the palette and the swatch rows, is restored — the picker offers seven distinct colours again.

D1 — the app icon having no glyph-only mark — is closed, and it was never really open. The mark exists: Design/logos/icon/ holds 23 glyph-only files, icon_white_<accent> and icon_black_<accent> for each of the seven accents, plus hollow variants. The blocker was raised against the eight full lockups this pass was handed, which are the only files in the set that could not deliver it. Nothing needs drawing and nothing needs requesting of anybody; board 83 draws from the icon set.
