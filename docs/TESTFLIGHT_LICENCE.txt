4. WHAT THE APP DOES WITH WHAT YOU WRITE

Outpost has no server of ours. What you write is encrypted on your device and written into
your own iCloud storage, addressed to the people you wrote it to, until their devices
collect it. It counts against your iCloud storage quota, not ours.

A photo or a video clip goes the same way. It is scaled down and stripped of its location
and camera information on your device, encrypted there, and written into your own iCloud
storage as a separate file until collected. It counts against your iCloud storage quota
in the same way.

Because content is encrypted before it leaves your device, we cannot read it, produce it
in response to a legal request, restore it, or delete it from anyone else's device.


5. WHAT WE CAN SEE, STATED PRECISELY

We do not operate a server, and the app contains no analytics, no advertising identifiers
and no third-party SDKs. We do not receive your messages, your posts, your room names,
your display name, or the names of the people you talk to.

We are not claiming that nothing is observable anywhere. Apple operates iCloud and APNs,
holds the encrypted material, and can see coarse delivery information — roughly, that one
account left something for another, when, and how large it was. Apple cannot read the
content, and neither can we. That distinction is the substance of the claim and is stated
here precisely rather than in summary.

Your use of iCloud and TestFlight is also subject to Apple's own terms.


6. NOTIFICATION PREVIEWS

If you switch on message previews in notifications, the decrypted preview text is written
into iOS's own notification store, which is outside the app's control and not covered by
the app's encryption. Content recovered from that store has survived app deletion in other
products. Previews are off by default; turning them on accepts that specific exposure.

8. HISTORY, MEMBERSHIP AND REMOVAL

Admitting someone to a room gives them that room's entire history from its first message.
Removing someone stops them receiving anything new; it does not and cannot retrieve what
they already hold, because that content is on their device and always was. Deleting a
message removes it from participating devices that behave honestly, and nowhere else.

The application states each of these at the point at which it applies. You acknowledge that
you understand them.

10. REPORTING AND BLOCKING

You may block any member unilaterally, and you may report any message to us from within

carries the message itself or any photo, and the app cannot attach them.

The app sends nothing itself. It copies the report and opens
https://outpostmessaging.com/report in your browser, where you paste it, choose a category
and leave a way to be reached. A person reads every one. If you leave an email address and
do not turn the option off, you are told what was done about it and whether our copy has
been deleted. We will not tell the person you reported, ever.
